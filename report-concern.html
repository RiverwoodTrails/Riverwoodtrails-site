Report a Concern

See something that doesn't look right — a garbage can screen out of compliance, a vehicle parked too long, a fence that needs a look? Let the board know here. This isn't for design review submissions — use the Design Review Application for those.

What happens after you submit

Your report goes directly to the board at home@riverwoodtrails.com. We'll follow up with the homeowner as needed — please allow 1–2 weeks for a response. Reports are not anonymous; we may need to contact you for details.

Prefer to email directly? Reach the board at home@riverwoodtrails.com.

CONTACT THE BOARD

Email us directly at home@riverwoodtrails.com or send a message below. General questions, design review requests, and neighborhood concerns are all welcome here.
